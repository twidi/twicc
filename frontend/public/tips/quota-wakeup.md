---
title: "Get an extra 5-hour quota window"
providers_any: [claude_code, codex]
---

Open your provider's **5-hour quota window** before work starts: set a daily **Quota wake-up** time in **Settings → Usage**.

TwiCC sends a small request at that time, so your next reset comes earlier in your workday.

TwiCC must be running. It skips the request if a window is already active.
