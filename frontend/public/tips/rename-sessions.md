---
title: "Name sessions your way"
---

Turn automatic first titles and updates off with **Settings → Title
suggestion → Automatic titles**. Manual suggestions remain available when
title generation is enabled.

To name a session yourself, click the **pencil** in the session header, or the
**Rename** entry in its **⋮** menu in the sidebar.

**Save** validates your title and freezes automatic updates, even when you
keep the same text. Explicit CLI or MCP renames do the same. Existing titles
from before automatic updates also stay frozen.

The dialog identifies automatic titles and explains Save. That hint stays
hidden while your own title is pending. Server updates do not replace text
you type in the open dialog. **Cancel** changes nothing.
